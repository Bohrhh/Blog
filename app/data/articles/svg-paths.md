# An Interactive Guide to SVG Paths

SVG gives us many different primitives to work with, but by far the most powerful is the `<path>` element.

## Understanding the d Attribute

The d attribute contains a series of commands:

```svg
<path d="M10 10 H 90 V 90 H 10 Z" />
```

## Common Commands

- M - Move to
- L - Line to
- H - Horizontal line
- V - Vertical line
- Z - Close path
