# The Post-Developer Era

When OpenAI released GPT-4 back in March 2023, they kickstarted the AI revolution.

## The Predictions

The consensus online was that front-end development jobs would be totally eliminated within a year or two.

## The Reality

Well, it's been more than two years since then, and things are quite different!

## What We've Learned

AI is a tool, not a replacement. Developers who learn to use AI effectively will thrive.

## Conclusion

Embrace the change!
